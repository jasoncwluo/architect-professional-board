## Core proposal

Develop architects who can translate business ambition into coherent, trusted and adoptable enterprise, data and AI solutions, and demonstrate their judgement through practical evidence.

Use the Top 10 as the common applied foundation, the Top 15 as the initial programme framework and the full 20 areas as a development catalogue. These are cumulative design groupings, not a validated ranking or twenty courses of equal length.

## Reading conventions

- **Proposed:** a design recommendation requiring validation.
- **Illustrative:** an example, not a mandated schedule or standard.
- **To validate:** an unresolved decision before formal adoption.

The underlying survey evidence is not included. This package is a generalised proposal, not a reproducible public analysis or a claim of verified individual skills gaps. No raw survey, respondent identifiers, customer examples or internal product observations are included.

## Review questions

- Are the role contributions and boundaries appropriate?
- Do the areas reflect important architecture decisions?
- Is the common foundation proportionate to role-specific depth?
- Can the proposed evidence be assessed fairly?
- What access, workload and management support would application require?
