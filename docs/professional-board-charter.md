# Professional Board charter — proposed

## Purpose

Maintain a coherent architect capability framework and assure fair, evidence-based development and assessment across the three pathways.

## Proposed responsibilities

- Maintain capability definitions and role-depth expectations.
- Approve and review assessment principles, rubrics and calibration.
- Review curriculum relevance, evidence quality and progression recommendations.
- Maintain transparent reassessment and appeal arrangements.
- Identify recurring structural barriers and escalate to accountable owners.
- Review the framework as practice and technology evolve.

## Explicit exclusions

The Board is not a customer/project architecture approval board. It does not replace business risk acceptance, delivery governance, line management, product ownership or every learning-delivery function. It cannot award official credentials without a separately approved mandate.

## Membership and decision rights

Propose representation from Enterprise, Data and AI architecture, learning design, delivery and relevant assurance functions. Appoint a chair and a programme secretariat. Names, quorum, terms, delegated authority and decision rules remain to be agreed.

Declare conflicts of interest. For disputed formal decisions, use reviewers independent of the original decision where practicable. Document rationale and apply agreed confidentiality controls.

## Operating cadence

During the pilot, review readiness at launch and evidence at completion and follow-up. Confirm a proportionate ongoing cadence after the pilot rather than prescribing a permanent meeting schedule now.

## Outputs

Versioned framework, assessment guidance, calibration records, approved changes, unresolved decisions and structural-remediation escalations. Individual evidence and personnel decisions must be kept in an appropriately controlled system, not this public repository.
