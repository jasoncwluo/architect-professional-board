# Evidence and publication boundaries

## Evidence status

A non-public survey of existing architects informed the initial challenge analysis. The underlying dataset and analytical audit trail are not included here. This repository therefore presents a generalised working proposal, not independently reproducible survey results, a representative workforce study or verified individual skills gaps.

Survey scenarios can reveal difficult situations, successful existing practice, requested resources and structural constraints. They do not by themselves establish a training deficit. Earlier exploratory theme detection had not been independently double-coded or validated as a professional standard.

The revised enablement areas merge and split earlier themes. Do not reuse earlier theme percentages as counts for the revised areas. A controlled reanalysis would need an explicit codebook, respondent-level deduplication and independent review.

The AI pathway requires dedicated practitioner validation; no separately labelled AI Architect survey cohort establishes its specialist requirements.

## Excluded from this package

- Original Excel and analytical workbook.
- Respondent counts, cohort comparisons and detailed statistics.
- Names, email addresses, response IDs and individual records.
- Direct quotations and identifiable customer examples.
- Internal product observations and roadmap claims.
- Personnel assessment results or credential records.

Absence of identifiers does not automatically establish publication permission. Review the generalised material with appropriate owners before publication; disclaimers and public repository ownership do not replace clearance.

## Evidence versus proposal

The framework, ordering, pathways, Board design, illustrative schedule and assessment approach are proposals. They are not approved job definitions, demonstrated programme effects or established certification requirements.

## Learning-design references

The following public sources informed the general learning-design recommendations, not the survey findings or role requirements:

- CDC, Building an Evaluation Plan: https://www.cdc.gov/training-development/php/about/evaluate-training-building-an-evaluation-plan.html
- The role of work environment in training sustainment: a meta-analysis: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0018720819845988
- The science of effective learning with spacing and retrieval practice: https://www.nature.com/articles/s44159-022-00089-1

These sources do not validate this programme or prescribe its role profiles, time allocations, follow-up intervals or assessment thresholds.

## Future evidence work

If authorised, maintain a restricted companion containing the source register, coding rules, review records, aggregation logic and limitations. Keep it separate from this public-facing package. Any future public statistics require explicit publication review.
