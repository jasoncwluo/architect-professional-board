# Role pathways

These are working learning profiles, not approved job descriptions or authority assignments. Validate them with role owners. Shared literacy does not require identical engineering depth. AI Architect expectations need dedicated practitioner validation; they are not established by a separate survey cohort.

## Enterprise Architect

**Purpose:** connect business direction, enterprise capabilities, investment choices and transformation governance.

**Decisions to practise leading within an agreed mandate:** capability priorities, transition states, cross-domain trade-offs and architecture governance design.

**Collaboration:** Data and AI Architects for specialist implications; business owners for priorities and benefits; delivery leaders for execution constraints.

**Learning emphasis:** capability/process architecture, value cases, roadmap dependencies, operating models, governance and executive advisory.

**Portfolio:** capability map, value hypothesis, transition roadmap, governance model and executive brief.

**Boundary:** does not replace business investment authority or personally perform every specialist design and assurance activity.

## Data Architect

**Purpose:** design trustworthy, usable and governed data foundations and context for business, analytics and AI consumers.

**Decisions to practise leading within an agreed mandate:** data architecture options, semantics, information flows, quality requirements, access patterns and modernisation choices.

**Collaboration:** business data owners/stewards for meaning and accountability; Enterprise Architects for direction; AI Architects for use-case-specific context and evaluation dependencies.

**Learning emphasis:** master data, semantics, ownership, data products, integration/replication/federation trade-offs, lineage, quality, platform coexistence and economics.

Ontologies and knowledge graphs are candidate techniques, not mandatory answers to every context problem. Validate required depth against scenarios.

**Portfolio:** data/context architecture, ownership proposal, data-product specification, quality/access requirements and modernisation decision record.

**Boundary:** does not unilaterally own business data policy or guarantee AI behaviour through data design alone.

## AI Architect

**Purpose:** qualify and design AI capabilities within business processes, data landscapes and enterprise controls.

**Decisions to practise leading within an agreed mandate:** AI suitability, pattern selection, model/retrieval/tool dependencies, evaluation approach, action boundaries and production readiness.

**Collaboration:** Data Architects for trusted context; Enterprise Architects for coherence; business owners for acceptable outcomes; security and operations specialists for assurance and lifecycle controls.

**Learning emphasis:** conventional automation versus predictive/generative/agent approaches, grounding, evaluation, permissions, human oversight, monitoring, change control, recovery and economics.

**Portfolio:** use-case assessment, AI architecture, evaluation plan, risk/control model and readiness recommendation.

**Boundary:** does not replace business risk acceptance, data ownership or specialist security authority.

## Shared proficiency language

| Level | Proposed expectation |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Explain concepts, recognise issues and identify when help is needed |
| Practitioner | Apply methods independently within an agreed scope and defend trade-offs |
| Lead | Guide complex work, challenge assumptions and assure decisions and development of others |

Define behaviour-specific descriptors and evidence before using these labels for formal progression.

## Entry routes

Use a work sample, practical diagnostic and development conversation to determine participation. Routes may include transition into architecture, cross-domain broadening, targeted technology refresh and Lead-level practice development. Recognise demonstrated competence rather than requiring identical attendance.
