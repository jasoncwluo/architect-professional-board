# Executive proposal

## Purpose

Develop architects who connect business ambition to coherent architecture, make and defend sound decisions, establish appropriate safeguards and help organisations adopt new capabilities successfully.

## Executive message

We propose a coordinated development programme for Enterprise, Data and AI Architects, supported by an Architect Professional Board. It combines a shared professional foundation, differentiated role pathways and practical assessment.

The intended result is not simply additional training or certification. It is demonstrable architectural capability, supported by the experience, tools and organisational conditions needed to apply it.

## Case for the proposal

The following messages are a synthesis informing programme design, not population estimates or findings independently reproducible from this public package.

| Design concern | Proposed response |
|---|---|
| Technical knowledge alone does not cover the intended architect contribution | Develop business framing, judgement, influence and technical depth together |
| Enterprise, data and AI decisions must connect | Shared foundations, explicit role contributions and joint exercises |
| Awareness and application are different | Coached practice, feedback, work evidence and individual defence |
| Not every barrier is a learning problem | Separate capability development from structural remediation |
| Technology-specific guidance changes | Maintained implementation resources alongside enduring principles |

## One programme, three pathways

- **Common foundation:** value, design judgement, assurance, influence and professional practice.
- **Enterprise Architect:** executable transformation across capabilities, investments and architecture domains.
- **Data Architect:** trusted data foundations, meaning, ownership and consumption context.
- **AI Architect:** appropriate AI use, evaluated behaviour and controlled production operation.
- **Joint assessment:** a coherent recommendation with explicit role contributions.
- **Board:** maintain expectations and assure assessment quality.

These role boundaries and responsibilities are proposals, not approved job definitions.

## Intended outcomes

Participants should demonstrate that they can frame the right problem; compare options; connect enterprise, data and AI decisions; identify readiness gaps; engage specialists; design for adoption; defend recommendations; and revise decisions when assumptions change.

Attendance, completion and confidence are supporting indicators, not evidence of competence by themselves. Business outcomes require careful evaluation and cannot automatically be attributed to the programme.

## Sponsor decision requested

Approve a bounded pilot to validate the capability model, pathways and assessment approach before broader rollout. Confirm:

1. Accountable sponsor and programme owner.
2. Role representatives and provisional Board mandate.
3. Pilot audience and access to practical application opportunities.
4. Protected time, mentors, assessors and environments.
5. Evidence-handling and publication boundaries.
6. Review criteria before expansion or formal certification.

Do not approve permanent certification thresholds or a fixed curriculum at this stage. Cost, capacity and duration remain to be estimated.
